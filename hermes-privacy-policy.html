Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Josh Hackett ("we," "our," or "us") manages information for the Josh Hackett Hermes SMS (Hermes) internal notification tool.

Data Collection

Hermes processes phone numbers solely provided by the system owner, Josh Hackett, to deliver system alerts. We do not collect, scrape, rent, or buy third-party user data.

Data Use

Hermes uses the provided mobile number only to send low-volume administrative notifications, task reminders, approval prompts, queue alerts, and system status updates to the system owner.

SMS Consent Data

SMS consent is optional and separate from other data collection. Mobile information and text messaging opt-in consent collected for SMS notifications will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share SMS-related information with service providers only as necessary to deliver the Hermes SMS notification program, subject to confidentiality and use restrictions. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from any unrelated data sharing.

SMS Program Controls

Message frequency varies, up to 30 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.
