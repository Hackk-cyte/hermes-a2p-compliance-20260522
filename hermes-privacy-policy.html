Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Josh Hackett ("we," "our," or "us") manages information for the Hermes internal notification tool.

Data Collection

Hermes processes phone numbers solely provided by the system owner, Josh Hackett, to deliver system alerts. We do not collect, scrape, rent, or buy third-party user data.

Data Use

Hermes uses the provided mobile number only to send low-volume administrative notifications, task reminders, approval prompts, queue alerts, and system status updates to the system owner.

SMS Consent Data

Mobile information/consent collected for SMS notifications will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the transfer or sharing of information excludes text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
